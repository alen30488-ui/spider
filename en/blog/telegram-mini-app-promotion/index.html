How to attract users to a Telegram Mini App

Where to get users for a Telegram Mini App: your own channel, the main Mini App in the bot profile, Telegram ads, sponsored posts, a referral program and tagged links. How to retain your audience and which metrics to track.

Switch Lab team Published October 25, 2026 4 min read

  2. How to retain users
  3. Which metrics to track

A Mini App cannot be found through Google or Yandex search, so you need a user acquisition plan before launch. The good news is that inside Telegram there are many free and inexpensive ways to bring in an audience — and users can open the app in one tap, without installation or registration. Here are eight channels that work and the rules that help retain users.

8 ways to attract users

  1. Your own channel and chats. The cheapest source. A post with a button that opens the Mini App, a pinned post in the channel and a link in the description.
  2. The menu button and the main Mini App. The bot's menu button opens the app right away. The main Mini App is shown in the bot's profile with a launch button and screenshots — like a page in an app store.
  3. Telegram ads. The official ad platform shows ads in channels, and they can lead straight to the bot.
  4. Sponsored posts in niche channels. A post with a direct link to the Mini App in channels your audience reads. It works well with a bonus for new users.
  5. Tagged links. A link like t.me/your_bot?startapp=autumn_promo opens the main Mini App and passes a parameter. It shows which channel, post or blogger brought in the user — and lets you immediately show the right screen or promo code.
  6. A referral program. A bonus for an invited friend and a “Share” button right in the app. Mini Apps that accept Telegram Stars can also use built-in affiliate programs: affiliates get a percentage of purchases made by the users they bring in.
  7. A home screen shortcut. A Mini App can offer to add a shortcut to the phone's home screen — so users can return to the app without opening the chat.
  8. Offline locations. A QR code at the checkout, on a table, on packaging or in a salon opens the Mini App right away, without searching for the bot.

How to retain users

Bringing a person in is half the job. The other half is making them come back.

  • Bot notifications only when there is a reason. An order status, a booking reminder, a personal offer. A promotional broadcast every day is a quick way to get the bot blocked.
  • A fast first screen. Mini Apps are often opened on mobile internet: if the first screen takes longer than a couple of seconds to load, many people will close the app.
  • Short onboarding. One screen with the main action is better than five slides about features.
  • Bonuses and progress. A loyalty program, streaks, levels and rewards give people a reason to return.
  • Saved settings. The app should remember the city, the delivery address and favorite products.

In the Mini App for the Fluent Club online school, reminders in Telegram instead of emails, together with game mechanics, helped double day-30 retention.

Which metrics to track

MetricWhat it shows
Launches by sourceWhich channels and posts bring in users — based on the link parameter
ActivationWhat share of new users completed the key action
Day 1, 7, 30 retentionWhether users come back
Conversion to paymentHow many users became buyers
Bot blocksWhether you are overloading your audience with notifications

Without these numbers, it is impossible to know where to invest the budget. We build link tags and analytics events in at the development stage.

Common mistakes

  • Launching without an audience. The app is ready, but there is no channel, no ad budget and no plan for the first weeks.
  • Links without tags. The money for sponsored posts has been spent, but nobody knows which of them worked.
  • Notification spam. Users block the bot, and there is no way to get them back.
  • Long onboarding. A Mini App does not need registration, yet it is still made five steps long.
