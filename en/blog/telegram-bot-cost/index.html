How much does a Telegram bot for business cost

How much a turnkey Telegram bot costs: for requests and FAQs from ₽30,000, a corporate bot from ₽60,000, for sales and bookings from ₽80,000, with a CRM and admin panel from ₽150,000. What affects the price, which costs follow the launch and when a bot builder is enough.

Telegram botsfrom ₽30,000 · from 1 week

Prices for Telegram bots differ by orders of magnitude: you can find offers for ₽5,000 and for half a million. The difference lies in what the bot can do, which systems it is connected to and who will be responsible for it after launch. Here is how much different bots cost, what the price consists of and how not to overpay.

What affects the cost

  • Scenarios. How many dialog branches, questions and answer options there are. A bot with three buttons and a bot that guides a customer from choosing a service to paying are different projects.
  • Integrations. CRM, 1C, Google Sheets, a booking system. Each integration means two-way data exchange and error handling. More on this in the article “Telegram bot and CRM integration”.
  • Payments. A payment provider or Telegram Stars, receipts, refunds, server-side amount checks. How it works is covered in the article “How to accept payments in a Telegram bot”.
  • Admin panel. Whether your team needs to change texts, process orders and launch broadcasts without a programmer.
  • Mini App. If chat buttons are not enough for a catalog or a calendar, a full interface is added to the bot.
  • Broadcasts. Telegram limits the speed of bulk sending to about 30 messages per second. Broadcasting to tens of thousands of users requires a queue, otherwise some messages will not get through.
  • Roles. Customers, employees and administrators see different menus and data.

If you need a simple auto-responder or a business card bot, a builder may be enough — we will tell you so honestly. Custom development pays off when the bot accepts payments, is connected to a CRM, works with customer data or becomes an important sales channel.

Costs after launch

  • Telegram — free: the platform does not charge for running a bot.
  • Server — a VPS or cloud, usually from a few hundred to a few thousand rubles a month, depending on the load.
  • Paid services — SMS, maps, AI models and others — at their own rates.
  • Payment fees — according to your payment provider's plan.
  • Support and improvements — per task or on a monthly plan from ₽20,000 a month.

How to save

  • Start with the main scenario. Requests or bookings right away; broadcasts and a loyalty program in the second stage.
  • Prepare texts in advance. Answers to common questions, service descriptions and prices are best collected before development starts.
  • Use what you already have. Your CRM can replace a separate admin panel.
  • Do not build a Mini App unless you need one. If buttons are enough for the customer, a bot will cost less.

How we estimate the cost

The work goes in stages: the scenario takes 1–3 days, a prototype in Telegram from 3 days, development and integrations from 1 week, the launch 1–2 days. After working through the scenario, we name the exact price and fix it in the contract. The bot runs on a server that we set up and connect to monitoring, and the code and access stay with you.

A good bot pays off quickly: in the bot for FixPoint service centers, the number of requests grew 2.3 times in three months, while incoming calls almost halved. If you are still choosing a format, read the article “Bot, Mini App or website — which one does your business need?”.
