Telegram booking bot: how it works and what it can do

How a Telegram bot for online booking at a salon, service center, clinic or studio works: choosing a service, specialist and time, reminders, rescheduling and cancellation, prepayment and CRM integration. When a bot is enough and when a Mini App is better for bookings.

Booking by phone means missed calls, an administrator who answers the same questions all day long and customers who forget about their appointment. A Telegram booking bot solves all three problems: customers book themselves at any time, and the bot reminds them of their visit and frees up the administrator's time. Here is how it works.

Why book in Telegram

  • The customer does not need to call during business hours and wait for an answer.
  • The administrator does not spend the day messaging — the bot shows free slots by itself.
  • Reminders get through: they arrive in the messenger the customer opens every day, not in their email.
  • Booking works around the clock, including at night and at weekends, when nobody answers the phone.

In the bot for the FixPoint service center chain, 70% of bookings now go through without an administrator, and the number of requests grew 2.3 times in three months.

What booking looks like

  1. Service. The customer chooses what they need and immediately sees the price and duration.
  2. Specialist. A specific specialist or “any available”.
  3. Date and time. The bot shows only free slots from the current schedule.
  4. Contacts. Name and phone number — just once, with the “Share contact” button, without typing.
  5. Confirmation. The customer receives a message with the booking details and the address.

The whole booking takes less than a minute, and on the next visit the bot already knows the customer.

Reminders and no-shows

No-shows are the main hidden loss for businesses that work by appointment. The bot helps reduce them:

  • A reminder a day before and a couple of hours before with “Confirm”, “Reschedule” and “Cancel” buttons.
  • A waiting list. If someone cancels, the bot offers the freed-up time to those who are waiting.
  • Prepayment or a deposit via YooKassa for expensive and long services.
  • After the visit, the bot asks the customer to rate the service and offers to book again.

We used similar mechanics in the app for FORMA fitness studios: reminders and a waiting list reduced missed workouts by 32%.

Connecting to the schedule and CRM

An important rule: the bot should not have its own schedule, separate from the one the administrators work with. Otherwise, two customers will book the same time. The bot takes free slots from your booking system, CRM or calendar and checks once again that the time is free before confirming.

Bookings go straight into the system, and managers see them together with the customer's history. Learn more about how this works in the article “Telegram bot and CRM integration”.

A bot is a good fit when there are only a few services and specialists. If customers need to compare specialists, look at photos of their work and choose from dozens of services, a Mini App is more convenient — a full interface inside Telegram. We explained what it is and how it works in the article “What is a Telegram Mini App”.

What to prepare for launch

  • a list of services with durations and prices;
  • the specialists' schedules and booking rules: how far in advance customers can book, whether they can choose a specialist;
  • cancellation and rescheduling rules;
  • texts for reminders and messages;
  • access to your booking system or CRM, if you already have one.
