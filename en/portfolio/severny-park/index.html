Severstroy Group

Website for the Severny Park residential quarter

A brand website with apartment search by parameters, interactive floor plans and a mortgage calculator, connected to the developer's CRM.

Client
Severstroy Group
Year
2024
Timeline
12 weeks
Platform
Web, responsive
1 / 3
  • +54%website leads
  • −31%cost per lead
  • 4.5 minaverage time on site

Challenge

The developer needed a website that sells: it should convey the atmosphere of the quarter, help find the right apartment quickly and pass hot leads to the sales team.

Solution

We built apartment search by rooms, price, area and floor, apartment pages with 2D/3D plans and window views, a mortgage calculator and online reservation. Prices and availability sync with Profitbase every 10 minutes.

What we did

  • Concept and visual style of the website
  • Apartment finder by rooms, price, area and floor
  • Apartment pages with 2D/3D plans and window views
  • Mortgage calculator with bank programs
  • Online apartment reservations
  • Prices and availability synced with Profitbase every 10 minutes

Result

Website leads grew by 54%, cost per lead dropped by 31%, and visitors spend 4.5 minutes on the site on average.
