ZERNO — Android app for the ZERNO coffee shop chain

Guests order coffee in advance and skip the line, collect bonus points and get personal offers from their favorite coffee shop.

Client
ZERNO
Timeline
11 weeks
Platform
Android

Result

Key results

Pre-orders took 27% of morning sales, repeat visits grew by 22%, and the average wait dropped to 3 minutes.

  • 27%of morning orders are pre-orders
  • +22%repeat visits
  • 3 minaverage wait

Challenge

What the client came with

Morning queues formed at the coffee shops, some guests walked away, and paper loyalty cards gave no customer data at all.

  • BeforeMorning queues at the till
  • BeforeSome guests left without ordering
  • BeforePaper loyalty cards, no guest data

Project goalRemove queues and bring guests back more often

Solution

What we did

We built an app with a menu and modifiers, pre-orders for a chosen time, real-time order status, a digital loyalty card and push promotions. Orders go straight to the iiko POS.

  • Menu with modifiers: milk, syrups, size
  • Pre-orders for a chosen time and coffee shop
  • Real-time preparation status
  • Digital loyalty card instead of a paper one
  • Personal push promotions
  • Orders sent to the iiko POS, YooKassa payments
  • Unity
  • C#
  • Go
  • PostgreSQL
  • iiko API
  • Firebase
  • YooKassa
