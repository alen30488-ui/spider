Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. General provisions

This policy explains how Switch Lab (“we”) processes the personal data of visitors to switchlab.dev. By submitting a request on the website, you confirm that you have read this policy and consent to the processing of your data.

2. What data we process

  • your name or how to address you;
  • contact details: Telegram, phone number or email;
  • project details you provide in the request;
  • technical data (IP address, browser type, cookies) if web analytics is enabled.

3. Why we process it

  • to contact you about your request and consult you;
  • to prepare a commercial proposal;
  • to conclude and perform a contract;
  • to improve the website.

4. Legal basis

We process data based on your consent and where necessary to conclude and perform a contract with you, in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

5. Storage and security

We keep data no longer than necessary for the purposes above and apply organizational and technical measures to protect it. We never sell personal data and do not share it with third parties, except for services that help us process your request and cases required by law.

6. Your rights

You may request access to your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time by emailing hello@switchlab.dev.

7. Cookies

The website may use cookies for proper operation and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings — this will not prevent you from sending a request.

8. Contact

For any questions about personal data processing, email hello@switchlab.dev or message us on Telegram at @switchlab.
