How we price

A price that doesn’t creep up along the way

  • 01Estimate after the brief

    We break the work into stages and price each one. “From” means the smallest real project, not a teaser.

  • 02Fixed in the contract

    Price, timeline and scope go into the contract. New ideas along the way get a separate estimate.

  • 03Milestone payments

    You pay for finished stages rather than everything upfront — and see the result at every demo.
