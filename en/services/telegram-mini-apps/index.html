A bot talks in messages and buttons, while a Mini App is a full interface: a photo catalog, filters, a cart, forms, a personal account. They usually work together: the bot notifies and brings customers back, the Mini App shows and sells.

Booking starts at ₽120,000, loyalty programs and customer accounts at ₽180,000, a store at ₽200,000, learning services and games at ₽350,000. The price depends on the number of screens, integrations and server-side logic.

No. A Mini App opens inside Telegram on iOS, Android and desktop — from a button in the bot or a direct link. No sign-up either: the user’s name and id come from Telegram.

Digital goods and services in Telegram are sold for Telegram Stars — that’s a platform rule. Physical goods and offline services can be paid by card via YooKassa and other providers. We can set up both.

Yes, as long as the server verifies the data signature from Telegram (initData) and never trusts prices or permissions coming from the interface. We build this in from day one and check existing Mini Apps as part of a security audit.
