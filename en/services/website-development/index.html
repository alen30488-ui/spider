Ask a question

A landing page starts at ₽50,000, a corporate website at ₽120,000, an online store at ₽250,000 and a web app at ₽350,000. The final price depends on the number of pages, design complexity and integrations. See “How much does a website cost” for the full breakdown.

A landing page takes 2–3 weeks, a corporate website 4–6 weeks and an online store 6–12 weeks. Every stage has a deadline in the contract.

We build on a modern stack — Next.js, Nuxt, Laravel — with a convenient CMS. Such a site is faster, more secure and isn’t limited by a website builder. If a builder is enough for your task, we’ll tell you honestly.

Every website ships with a technical SEO foundation: fast loading, a mobile version, meta tags, structured data, a sitemap and connected Search Console and Yandex Webmaster. Rankings then depend on content and competition — we’ll tell you what to do after the launch.

We can write the copy, pick photos and upload products. If you prepare the content yourself, we’ll give you a structure and requirements and train you to use the CMS.

You do. We work under a contract: after payment you receive the source code and access to the hosting, domain and CMS, and all rights pass to you.
