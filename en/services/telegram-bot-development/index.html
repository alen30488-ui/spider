Ask a question

A lead and FAQ bot starts at ₽30,000, an internal team bot at ₽60,000, a bot with a catalog and payments at ₽80,000 and a bot with CRM and an admin panel at ₽150,000. We give an exact price once the flows are clear.

Yes. Physical goods and services are paid by card through providers such as YooKassa. For digital goods and services Telegram requires Telegram Stars. We’ll set up the right option and verify every payment on the server.

If a few buttons and messages are enough for your customers, a bot will do. If you need a photo catalog with filters, a complex form or a personal account, go for a Mini App — it opens inside Telegram like an app. See “Bot, Mini App or website” for examples.

On a server we set up for you — a VPS or cloud in Russia or abroad, whichever suits you. We add monitoring, so we know about an outage before your customers do.

Yes. We’ll dig into the code, fix bugs and add features. Before that we run a quick review so you don’t inherit vulnerabilities. See improvements & support.

A bot can’t message someone who hasn’t started it, and broadcasts are limited by Telegram — roughly 30 messages per second. We design flows and broadcasts around these rules so the bot doesn’t get restricted.
